Privacy policy

Data we collect

We only collect what you type into the contact form: name, work email, company, estimated budget and project description.

This information is used solely to answer your request and, where relevant, to prepare a proposal. It is never sold or used for advertising.

Transfers

The form is delivered by email through the FormSubmit service (formsubmit.co). The website's fonts are loaded from Google Fonts servers, which shares your IP address with Google during your visit.

Cookies

Retention

Our exchanges are kept for the duration of our relationship, then for at most three years after our last contact.
